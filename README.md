## Overview

SkillNest is a marketplace concept built around one idea: **students already have valuable skills — they just need a place to sell them.**

The platform is designed to give students an online storefront for their products and services, while helping customers discover, order and receive those services with confidence.

## The problem

Many students in Rwanda offer design, writing, tech support, translation, photography and other services, but they rely on scattered WhatsApp statuses, word of mouth and informal arrangements. There is no simple space that brings their offers together, builds trust and supports the full journey from order to delivery.

## Planned features

- 🏪 Student storefronts with profiles, skills and service listings
- 🛍️ Product and service catalogue with search and categories
- 📦 Order management and delivery coordination
- 💳 Digital payment support
- ⭐ Reviews and trust signals
- 📁 Business portfolio building for students
- 📊 Simple dashboard for sellers to manage their activity

## Tech direction

The first version will focus on a lightweight, responsive web experience:

- **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** for the front-end
- **Git and GitHub** for version control
- Mobile-first layouts, because most students browse on their phones

The back-end and payment stack will be chosen after the prototype is validated.
